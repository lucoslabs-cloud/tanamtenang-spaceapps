# Super Intelligence (SI) & AI Attribution

This document outlines the scope, tools, and boundaries of Super Intelligence (SI) and generative AI utilised in the development of **TanamTenang** for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (*Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data*), in compliance with challenge submission guidelines.

---

## 1. Tools Used
- **Audio & Voice Synthesis:** [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) (used for narration in the project demonstration video).
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Anthropic Claude & Google Gemini (used for engineering acceleration, algorithmic porting, and documentation refinement).

---

## 2. Where SI Was Utilised

### Audio & Narration (ElevenLabs)
- **Demo Video Voiceover:** The spoken narration in the 30-second project demonstration video was synthesized using ElevenLabs.
- **Attribution & Metadata:** In accordance with NASA Space Apps guidelines (*"Audio: Must acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and metadata"*), the video description, project documentation, and metadata explicitly credit ElevenLabs for the voice generation.

### Code & Engineering Acceleration (LLMs)
- **Algorithmic Porting:** Assisting in translating the FAO Soils Bulletin 32 trapezoidal membership equations, Liebig's law calculations, and rotation search logic from the backend PHP/PostGIS platform into client-side TypeScript.
- **Frontend & Worker Scaffolding:** Assisting in scaffolding Vue 3 components, Tailwind CSS v4 styling, MapLibre GL canvas layers, and off-thread Web Worker messaging.
- **Documentation:** Assisting in structuring technical summaries, markdown tables, and editorial refinement of user guides.

---

## 3. What SI Was NOT Used For (Original Human & Scientific Foundation)

- **No Synthetic Earth Observation Data:** No climate, elevation, soil, or vegetation figures were generated or simulated by AI. All data originates strictly from official NASA sources (SRTM, POWER, MODIS, NEX-GDDP-CMIP6) and partner databases (ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0, UN OCHA).
- **Original Agronomic Model & System Architecture:** The decision matrix, 4-strategy rotation formulation (Carry On, Best Return, Build Soil, Conserve Water), 343 enterprise requirement envelopes, and "grid-as-an-image" architecture were designed, formulated, and verified by the team.
- **No NASA Branding Elements:** In accordance with challenge rules, no NASA logos, flags, seals, or mission identifiers were created, modified, or generated by SI tools.
